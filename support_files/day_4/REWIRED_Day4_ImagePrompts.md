## STYLE GUIDE (Apply to all prompts)

**Base style:** Cinematic anime-adjacent illustration, semi-realistic proportions, muted color palette with warm undertones. Soft lighting, textured linework. NOT generic anime. Think Makoto Shinkai backgrounds meets indie visual novel character art. Atmospheric, moody, grounded in realism.

**MC appearance:** Young woman, early 20s, dark brown hair past shoulders (slightly messy/natural), brown eyes, freckles across nose and cheeks, slim build, 5'6". Fair skin, no makeup. Looks like a college student. Expressive but understated.

**Color palette:** Warm neutrals, muted navy, dusty rose, soft gold afternoon light. Interiors lean cooler (blue-grey tones). Exteriors lean warmer.

## NOTES

- All images should avoid showing MC's face in explicit detail during sexual/intimate scenes. Use angles, lighting, partial framing.
- Maintain consistent character design across all images (hair color, freckles, build, clothing).
- Background detail should feel lived-in and slightly messy, never sterile or perfect.
- Lighting is critical to mood. Morning = soft warm. Afternoon = golden dappled. Evening = deep warm amber. Night = cool blue-grey with phone/laptop glow.
- If generating with AI art tools, add negative prompts for "generic anime style, overly glossy, perfect skin, heavy makeup, unrealistic proportions."
