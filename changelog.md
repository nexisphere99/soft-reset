## Rewired v0.5 - "Re-Entry" is LIVE 🦋

Hey everyone 💜

Rewired v0.5 is live. Day 5 is playable. She goes back to work. Owen sees her for the first time, five deliveries later she has her best tip day ever, and on the long way home she finds a coffee shop that feels like somewhere she could be a person.

📥 Download (HTML): [link]

What's new:

📅 Day 5 - Her first full workday as a woman. A shower, her first ponytail, and a DashDrop jacket that finally fits. Five deliveries, a ride through downtown, a latte in a corner booth, and a phone call from Nolan she can't dodge anymore.

🛵 Back at DashDrop - Owen's brain visibly buffering, a new ID slid across the counter, and a manager who decides the kindest thing he can do is pretend it's normal. Five deliveries across the city, and every doorstep treats her differently than it used to. Best shift she's ever had, and she knows exactly why.

👥 6 new NPCs - Trent (the hot couple's guy, who tips five dollars more than he ever tipped Marcus, plus his girlfriend Nina), Sage (a yoga instructor who thinks she needs a stretch), Kevin (a very stoned, very friendly college kid), Jess (a tired single mom and her toddler), River Chen (the barista at Grounded, and a main romance option), and Owen, updated.

☕ Grounded Coffee House - A new location in Northgate. Chalkboard menu, exposed brick, a booth she'll keep going back to. Order a latte, a muffin, or River's hot chocolate. Come back later in the evening and he asks what she does, and she tells him she's carrying something heavy.

🏙️ Downtown, partly unlocked - Black Line Studio and Nocturne Bar are now visible in Central City. Neither is open to her yet, but the Help Wanted sign in Nocturne's window is noted. Sunrise Yoga's flyer is in her pocket.

📱 Her phone, properly - Jordan texts again and you can set up a Friday date, stall, or let him sit on read. Nolan sends sixteen messages in forty minutes. A dating app profile gets 47 likes, 12 matches and 6 messages in eleven minutes, and one of them is a firefighter named Chris who seems almost suspiciously normal. Plus a selfie, and a late night scroll through Instagram where she starts studying how women present themselves.

🎲 Random encounters - A man watching from a bus shelter, a scooter that dies mid route and a stranger who knows how to fix it, Helen and her dog Biscuit in the snack aisle, Hector calling her "mija", and a cellist on a corner playing Bach to nobody.

🔥 More choices on the side - Trent's hallway invitation, Kevin's party, a quiet evening alone if the day has built up enough, and a lavender flyer on the fridge.

🛠️ Under the hood

- Dinner on Day 5, cooked or ordered, now leads into Nolan's call so the day can't get stuck.

## Rewired v0.4 - "First Public Day" is LIVE 🦋
16 October

Hey everyone 💜

Rewired v0.4 is live. Day 4 is playable. She goes outside for real this time, not a grocery run in a panic, an actual Monday. A stranger holds a door for her. A guy chases her down the sidewalk to tell her she's pretty. Hector doesn't recognize her at all.

📥 Download (HTML): https://pixeldrain.com/u/hund8Tgf

What's new:

📅 Day 4 - The first day she's out in the world as herself with nowhere to hide. A shower and her first real leg shave, two honest nicks included. Getting dressed, actually dressed, French tuck and all. A full grocery run at the corner store under a name Hector's never heard. Cooking dinner and sitting down to eat it like it's normal, because today it almost is.

👋 First public day - No more cave days. This is the one where the apartment stops being the whole world and the sidewalk starts being real.

👥 3 new NPCs - Jordan (a guy who runs her down on the street just to say she's pretty, three different ways to handle that), Denise (the actual neighbor two doors down, thinks she's "Marcus's girlfriend"), and a dog named Maple who doesn't care about any of it and just wants to be pet

😏 Jordan, start to finish - Polite, firm, or give him your number. If you give him the number, he actually texts that night, and where it goes from there is on you, casual to flirty to a lot more than flirty.

📸 A first selfie - If things go far enough with Jordan, she takes her first photo of herself as Mia on purpose. Not for the mirror. For him.

🪒 Small firsts that aren't sexy but matter - A razor nick on the ankle. A French tuck that actually looks right. A walk that stops being a performance halfway through practicing it.

💭 The dream comes back - Same hallway of mirrors from two nights before, except this time the one in the red dress is closer, and when she wakes up she isn't crying.
