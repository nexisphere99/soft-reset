Marcus Cole is broke. Twenty years old, $23,400 in student loans, two months behind on rent, eating instant ramen for dinner because real food is a luxury. He delivers other people's meals on a dying electric scooter and goes home to a studio apartment with a water stain on the ceiling shaped like a fist.

Then he sees a flyer: $2,000. One pill. One visit.

NovaCure Laboratories. A pharmaceutical trial. He doesn't read the fine print. He doesn't ask what the pill does. He just needs the money.

He swallows it. Goes home. Falls asleep. Wakes up as a woman.

Rewired is an open-world life simulation about what happens after. Not the fantasy version - the real version. The panic attack on the bathroom floor. The first bra from a thrift store. The catcall that makes you understand something you never understood before. The orgasm that rewires everything you thought you knew about your own body.
